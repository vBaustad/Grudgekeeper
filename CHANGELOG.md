# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Search the settings.** A search box beside the page title finds a setting by its name, its help or a choice in its dropdown, wherever it lives. The results drop down under the box, each with the page it is on. Click one, or pick it with the arrow keys and Enter, to go there, and the row lights up for a moment. By dakristia.
- **A guildie's spree toast burns as yours does.** Its seal stamps in on fire, and from 20 kills the count slams on beside it (×20, ×25 and on). Its sounds go by your setting for guildies' sounds.
- **No YippYapp page.** Grudgekeeper no longer adds a YippYapp page to Options or a /yippyapp command. They came with a library it shares with other addons, and it never used them.
- **Under PvP in the AddOn list.** Grudgekeeper now sits in the PvP group of the game's AddOn list.

## 1.13.1

- **Told when a newer version is out.** When a guildie has a newer Grudgekeeper than yours, one line in chat says so: the version you have and the new one. It works from this version on, so you hear of the next one.
- **Versus hears your guildies' fights, however old.** A guildie said only what had happened since you were last on, so a fight from while you were away and they were on never reached you, and two of you saw different scores. Now each guildie online when you log in, and each who logs in while you are on, says their count against the guild your officers track, up to 40 of them, and their latest 20 fights against them, however old. The one who tells needs this version; for the older fights in the list, so do you.

## 1.13.0

- **Shame players wear a badge for each tag** over their nameplate, in the row with the Kudos medals: a tarnished medal with the tag's symbol, three at most. They stay in a fight, as a warning; Kudos' medals still go. By dakristia.
- **One player on both Shame and Kudos.** Someone can be a great healer and still ninja loot: giving kudos no longer takes them off Shame. Each list shows only its own tags, on the nameplate (Shame's row over Kudos'), in the tooltip and on their card, which lists each tag once with everyone who gave it and leads with the tab you opened it from. By dakristia.
- **A warning when someone in your Death Note targets you.** Out of a fight, and only one you can see: their nameplate has to be up. Once a minute for each, with a short sound of its own you can pick, Heartbeat to begin with. Off to begin with, under Settings, Warnings.
- **The test panel and the screenshot mode stay with us.** They were tools for testing and for the store page's pictures, and the download no longer has them. `/grudge debug` prints what the addon can see, for a bug report.
- **What you owe works with TSM's mailbox too.** With TSM's mail window, the card of bounties you owe never came, and gold you mailed from it was never marked paid. Now the card comes whatever window the mailbox opens in, and gold you mail from any of them pays. Its button, Fill in mail, fills the game's own window, which TSM has a button to switch to. The card shows the gold as coins.
- **A login hears what it missed.** A kill or a death was heard only by whoever was online, so after a while away the News and the Versus tab had none of it, and two guildies saw different scores. Now each guildie online answers your login with their own kills and deaths since you were last on (half a day at most) and their count against the guild your officers track, and none of it comes as a toast. A guildie who is offline when you log in tells you when they log in while you are on.
- **Quick setup.** The settings open on a page of two choices. **What you see** sets the rows of Mine, Guildies and Warnings at once: Standard, Everything, Kill orders, Only mine, Warnings only or Nothing on screen. Change a row yourself and it says Custom. **What you share** is what Mode was, by what each does: Everything, All but where I am, or Nothing.
- **What you share no longer decides what you see.** Streamer and Private also hid your guildies' notifications and their kill orders' letters, which no setting said. Now only your rows do that. If you had Streamer or Private, your guildies' toasts and the kill order letters are turned off once, so nothing new turns up, and you can turn any of them back on.
- **The right-click menu, a line at a time.** A page of its own in the settings has a box for each of our lines: on an enemy the Grudge file, the kill order, the Death Note and their guild for an officer; on a player on your side the profile, Shame, Kudos and their notes. It was one box for all of them, and if you had it off, every line stays off.
- **In the game is two pages,** Nameplates & map and Buttons & tabs, beside the Right-click menu.
- **The Death list is the Death Note.** The same list, with a new line at the top of its page.
- **One Death Note for all your characters, if you want it.** Off to begin with, on the settings' new Death Note page. On, every character of yours on the same side shares the names, your notes and the warning, so the ones you hate are there on the alt you level. Each character's own list joins it, and is back as it was if you turn it off.
- **One in your Death Note warns you even with Kill order target near off.** With a kill order on them, only the kill order's warning could tell you, so turning it off left them silent.
- **A death that ends a kill spree is one line in the News.** The News said both who fell to whom and who ended whose spree, so one death read as two. Now it is the one line: who ended whose spree.

## 1.12.0

- **Patch notes.** After an update the letter by the icon opens the patch notes, in the game's bronze frame and your skin, with a list of what is new and what changed. `/grudge news` opens them any time.
- **A Death list kill has its own effect.** The Death list's eye seal slams in, the eye opens and glows red, and a notch is cut in the wax for the kill. DEATH LIST stands over your splash; the toast plays it small. They stay on your list.
- **Grudgebound ×20 and up and a hundred in a row sound new,** so the first Grudgebound stands out.
- **`/grudge shots` takes Settings, Sharing, with Mode,** and no longer a title letter.
- **Ten open kill orders, and you want another?** The poster shows your ten, the oldest first, those your deaths posted marked so, each with Withdraw. Take one down and the poster is back. It used to say only "withdraw one first", without where.
- **No letter for a new title or a bounty paid.** A new title has its splash, and a bounty comes by mail or in a trade. Your own deaths' letter is for a death that posted a kill order, as before.
- **The mailbox's Fill in writes a line in the letter too,** with the name and the gold, as before: press Send.
- **The leaderboard's "resets in" is on This week only.** All time never resets, and no longer says it does.
- **The test panel plays each mode.** `/grudge debug`, By mode: Normal, Streamer, Private or Your settings plays each splash, toast and letter that mode lets through, once each, then opens the letters.
- **Death list: only who you put there.** A tab of your own for the enemies you picked. Add someone on the page, or use Add to Death list on their card or in the right-click menu. It is what Track was, with room for 100 in place of 25, and everyone you tracked is on it. Nobody leaves it unless you take them off. They wear the eye and warn you when near, as before.
- **Grudges is what came by itself, in place of Most wanted.** Killed me has who killed you, with the score and what you are owed. Killed us has who killed the guild most. Those you only killed no longer have a row there: they are on the You page while they are in your latest kills. Only mine is on Versus alone, as Killed me does that job. Forget grudge is a button of its own on the ledger's card, under the two others. The smaller card on the Inspect window leaves it to the ledger.
- **Kill streaks count no grey and no farmed kill.** A revenge, avenge or kill order bonus used to take one into the streak. The bonus points are still yours. Such a kill doesn't end a streak either. Best streaks from before stay as they are.
- **The automatic kill order reads as your own setting.** It is My deaths can post kill orders now, under Settings, Sharing, and says both of its rules: killed by someone 3 times in half an hour, or 3 of the guild killed by them in two hours. What it does is unchanged.
- **Notes on your Death list.** Add to Death list opens a small window in the middle of the screen: write a note or don't, then Add or Cancel. The note is on their row, and Edit on their card changes it. Only you see it. Killing one on your list is a toast of yours with no letter, unless it is a revenge or fulfils a kill order, which are told as before. The setting is Grudge or Death list kill, under Settings, Mine.
- **Ready for big guilds.** Past 150 open kill orders your own unpaid orders could be dropped with what you owed on them, and fulfilled orders could come back as open. Open and closed orders now have room of their own, and yours are never let go to make room. The leaderboard holds 1200 players in place of 500. When the letters are full a guildie's kill order goes first, not the letter about yours. With more than 25 online, a few guildies answer a login in place of everyone at once.
- **One window for kill orders.** The search is twice as wide, with the WANTED poster on its right: pick someone, set the bounty with the game's own boxes and Seal it. The window stays, and their row gets the seal, so the next one is a click. Add someone on the Death list uses the same window, with your note in the bounty's place.
- **Highlights on profiles are easier to read.** Assists, kill streak and orders fulfilled stand side by side, and the nemesis has a row with their face and how often.
- **Mode: Normal, Streamer or Private.** Under Settings, Guildies and Sharing. Streamer: nothing of your guildies' on screen - no toasts, kill order letters, warnings or map pins - but your own kill orders, and where you are is never sent - no zone with a kill, an assist, a death or a fulfilled order, and no spot on the map when you see a target. Private: only what is about you, and nothing shared - no score, no profile outside the guild, and no kill order posted by your death, while what is about you is still told. Kill orders you post, back or fulfil reach the guild in every mode.
- **Quieter kill orders.** A new order is a letter, a warning and a pin when it has gold on it, is a friend's or is about you: yours, backed by you, on your Death list or on someone who killed you. The others are only listed. This is so for everyone, also in Normal. The kill orders page has All, Bounties and Mine.
- **New settings pages, with the game's own controls.** They are dark in either skin, with dropdowns, checkboxes and red buttons, and more room between the rows. What shows and whether it sounds are on the same row. Your own, your guildies' and the warnings have a page each, and the Sounds page is gone. The presets are gone too: each row says it itself. In Arrange on screen the boxes are called Splash and Toasts, as they move every one of them.
- **Splashes are for you, toasts are for your guildies.** Your own moments are a splash, a toast or off, and a guildie's is a toast or off. A guildie's that was set to a splash is a toast now.
- **The splashes and toasts keep their own sounds, yours and your guildies' apart.** Each moment has a box for yours and one for a guildie's, on or off. A sound picked for one before goes back to its own, and None picked stays off, except for a spree ended, which started on None. Mine, Guildies and Warnings each have their own Play sounds: off, the sounds on that page are off and greyed out, and come back as they were when it is on again. With Sounds off before, all three start off. The ledger's and the letters' own sounds have a box under Settings, Appearance.
- **The sprees keep their surprises.** No step of a spree has a row of its own. Reaper is always the scythe: the choice of the smoke is gone.
- **Warnings with urgency.** Ten urgent sounds come first in the menu: Red alert, Alarm bells, Attack alarm, War call, Battle trumpet, Under attack, War horn rush, Ambush, Battle roar and Heartbeat. Each hits at once, from the game's own sounds played as one. The ten tamest of the others left the menu. One you had picked stays.
- **Sounds worth picking, for the warnings.** A kill order target near and a Shame player joining your group are the two sounds you pick, from horns, drums, bells, gongs, roars, blows and rumbles from the game, each kind under its own title, with other addons' sounds in a menu of their own. Each has a play button in the menu, so you can hear one after another without opening it again, and the last one stops as the next starts. The dull ones and the nearly silent ones are gone, and the voice lines are not on the list: a pick of one goes back to that warning's default. A picked sound that has gone missing plays a horn: before, it could be a chime that the game has at a hundredth of full volume. A Shame player joining sounds a troll gong to begin with, outside YippYapp.
- **War horn rush and the Alliance bell.** A kill order target near now plays War horn rush, and a Shame player joining your group the Alliance bell. Everyone is moved to these two once with this version; pick another under Settings, Warnings and it stays.
- **Kudos players wear medals** (by dakristia). On their nameplate: a painted medal for each of their kudos tags, up to three and a count of the rest, the one most people gave first, in place of the small dim mark. They hide while you are in combat and in dungeons and raids, give way to a kill order's seal and the eye over your Death list, and move up over a raid icon. Shame keeps its small mark, in combat too. The box for both is under Settings, In the game.
- **Screenshots are off.** The first betas took one at every kill order you fulfilled, and that stayed on for everyone who had them. It is switched off once for everyone. Tick it again under Settings, Appearance if you want it.
- **Us against them.** Grudges has a Versus tab for the enemy guild your officers track: what was Watch their guild is Track their guild now, and all of it is on that tab, no longer a line on the kill orders page. On the left are up to 25 of theirs who fought us, the most fought first, with how often we killed them and how often they killed us. On the right, two war banners: each guild's score and top killer, who leads, and the latest fights. A click on their banner shows who of theirs was seen lately, and a click on a row opens no card. With no guild tracked, the tab says how one is, and an officer tracks one, changes it or stops from there. A player counts once we know they are in that guild, seen with it while it is tracked or named so by a guildie, and so do the kills and deaths against them your client heard of before and still keeps. It keeps the latest 200 enemies each way and lets the tracked guild's players go last. Each client counts what it heard, so two guildies can see different numbers. Guildies on this version tell the guild which guild the player they killed or died to was in.
- **Marked is gone.** The guild's kill-on-sight list went with its tab on Most wanted, Mark someone, its red line on the tooltip, its mark on the nameplate and the warning when a Marked player was near. Kill orders do that job for the guild, and your Death list does it for you. A note alone puts nobody on a list now, and what the guild wrote about an enemy still shows on their poster and Grudge file. `/grudge notes` opens Shame now.
- **No warning any more when one who leads over you is near.** Instead, when you kill one who still leads over you, a toast says where you stand ("Grudge: ... now 2 - 3"), or "Even with ..." once you have caught up. A kill that is a Revenge keeps its own splash. It is under Settings, Mine, Grudge or Death list kill, and `/grudge spree grudge` shows it.
- **The kill orders page lists every open order.** It stopped at the 25 with the biggest bounties, while the minimap button counted them all, so 46 open orders showed as 25. Now every open order is on the page, turned over a page at a time. Grudges, the News and the leaderboard still show their first 25, and the Death list shows all of itself.
- **A bounty typed again replaces the old one.** Open a kill order you had put 1g on, type 1, and it became 11g: the new digit went in beside the old one. Now the first click in a gold or silver box selects what is there, so what you type replaces it. Where you have put gold in a guildie's pot already, the poster says what your share is and that a new amount replaces it, and the seal says Change it.
- **A changed bounty reaches those who were away.** When you changed the gold on your kill order or in a pot, took it out or withdrew the order, a guildie who was offline could keep the old amount. Now Grudgekeeper tells the guild again shortly after you log in, gold taken out stays out, and a change made just as someone logs in is no longer undone by the old amount. If the kill comes before the two of you have been online together, they still see the old amount. A character played on two PCs tells the guild again only when somebody else logs in, as before.

## 1.11.0

- **A tormentor put down slams the grudge book down.** When you put an end to an enemy who had killed several of your guild, the splash is no longer a black seal stamped PAID. The old, skull-bound book of grudges drops from above and strikes the ground: a flash, the screen shakes, a ring of dust runs out along the ground and dust and sparks burst from under it, then TORMENTOR SLAIN slams down over it and embers rise. A guildie's, when it comes as a toast, has the same book falling, smaller, without the words. `/grudge spree tormentor` shows both.
- **A spree's end comes and goes with its shatter.** Its words show with the burning seal from the start and turn grey as it bursts, and the splash or toast fades as the shards fall. Before, the words came only after the shatter and then stood on their own for the rest of the announcement time.
- **REVENGE stands where TORMENTOR SLAIN does.** On your revenge's splash the word now slams down above their face, in blood red, in the same lettering and size as TORMENTOR SLAIN and with the same flash, and the line comes up just under the bronze ring. Before, the word stood under the ring, smaller.
- **A fulfilled kill order is paid in gold or stamped.** When you get an order's target, the WANTED poster slaps up and its face is crossed out as before. Then, if there was gold in the pot, a fat purse of gold slams down onto the poster and the coins burst out of it, spinning; if there was none, a heavy brass stamp slams down, lifts away and leaves FULFILLED in red. KILL ORDER FULFILLED slams down above it, where TORMENTOR SLAIN stands, and the line under the poster says who got them and the gold. The dagger is gone. A guildie's, when it comes as a toast, has the same purse or stamp, smaller. `/grudge spree fulfilled` shows both.
- **A new title is struck with a hammer.** When you reach a new title, a big hammer comes swinging in and strikes with a heavy thud, a flash and a ring of dust, and where it struck stands the title's own emblem. Each of the eleven titles has one, in iron up to Slayer, bronze up to Reckoner and gold above, and the higher the title, the bigger the blow. The title's name slams down just above it and the line under it says the points. A guildie's, when it comes as a toast, has the title's emblem pop out of a little cloud of dust and blow it away, larger than the old seal, before the words fade in beside it. `/grudge rank all` shows every title in turn.
- **Heavier sounds for a spree's end, Reaper and Grudgebound.** They use the game's own sounds now, deep ones in place of the chimes. A spree's end rumbles with thunder as the seal pulses, rock bursts as it shatters and a tribal bell tolls, and the raid boss chime after it is gone. Reaper's scythe comes with a big whoosh, the tribal bell and a Mortal Strike's swing. Grudgebound's crown of chains comes with the ground shaking, a war horn and a chain snapping. Every fifth kill after it, the crown comes down with a big whoosh and lands with chains, and the number slams in with a heavy rock and the tribal bell. A hundred in a row shakes the ground through its pause, then slams in with chains and the bell before the voice. The tribal bell and a Horde war horn can be picked for any sound under Settings, Sounds.
- **A big moment's own sound comes with its blow.** The sound of a new title, a fulfilled kill order, a revenge and a tormentor put down now plays as the hammer, the purse or stamp, the knife or the book strikes, as one sound with the thud. Before, it came a moment after the blow, or as the splash appeared, before anything struck. A guildie's toast does the same. A fulfilled kill order's sound is the tribal bell now, where it was the game's PvP alert, and its poster slaps up with the sound of parchment laid down.
- **The grudge book lands harder.** A heavy rock impact plays with its thud, which was too deep to hear over the game's music.
- **The lines under splashes and toasts are in colour.** What was won is in gold: the kills, the points, the title, the bounty. A guildie's name is in their class's colour, and an enemy's too where their class is known, else in red.
- **The test panel plays every one.** `/grudge debug` has a column that plays every splash of yours one after another, every toast of a guildie's, every title, and each title on its own button. A kill order with no gold in its pot has buttons too.
- **A kill order is written on a WANTED poster.** Posting a kill order, or adding to a guildie's pot, opens their wanted poster, the same one that is crossed out when an order is fulfilled, where it was a scroll with thin lines to write on. Their face is in its window, their class on its corner, and their name and level under it. The bounty and the why are boxes printed in heavy ink, under a heading between thick bars, and Cancel is a box at the foot. On the poster every race has a painted face of its own, a man and a woman, both kinds of Skyborne too: a woman's where you have seen that they are one, a man's where nobody has. Someone whose race nobody knows shows the other side's first race in your own body type: an orc if you are Alliance, a human if you are Horde, a woman's face if you play a woman. The three sentences under the boxes are one line now: you pay by mail when it's fulfilled, and it runs 7 days. The X in its corner is gone: Cancel or Escape closes it.
- **A fulfilled kill order's poster wears the same painted faces.** The WANTED poster whose face is crossed out when an order is fulfilled shows the target's race in our own paint, as on the poster the order was written on, where it showed the game's race portrait. It shows their race even when nobody ever saw them, from the race the order names, where it showed their class, and when no race is known at all it shows the same face as the poster the order was written on.

## 1.10.0

- **A letter opens where it stays.** It slid a little down as it opened and then jumped back up. Now it fades in, in its place.
- **A fulfilled kill order is a WANTED poster.** When you get an order's target, the splash is their wanted poster, not the red seal: it slaps up with their face in its window, a dagger pins it, two strokes of red ink cross the face out and FULFILLED is stamped across it, with the line about who got them and the bounty under it. A guildie's, when it comes as a toast, has the same poster, smaller, crossed out and stamped, without the dagger. `/grudge spree fulfilled` shows both.
- **A letter holds still while you drag it.** It was drawn again whenever news came in from the guild, also while you dragged it, and its text could flicker. Now news waits until you let go, and the title and words sit on whole pixels.
- **A kill order's letter says who at a glance.** The name of whom it is about is larger, in a plain font with room above and below it, their level stands large and dark on the right of the head, in from the corner, and the bounty is larger. The line with their level, race and class is gone: their face and the class crest on its shoulder show those. Open in the ledger is no longer a button: click their face to open the order in the ledger. And every letter closes one way, with Close at its foot (or Escape): the X in its corner is gone.
- **Old kill orders no longer come back as new letters.** A guildie's client that did not know its realm yet passed some kill orders on with names like "Snik Toby-", and each guildie who passed one on again added a dash. Each spelling was taken for a new order, with a letter of its own: an old order came back, said "Posted by Snik Toby-'s death", showed no place, and never matched its target on a nameplate. Such names are now read as the guildie's own, so a passed-on order is the one you already have. Once, at login, the copies already kept are merged into their order, the letters they brought go, and a guildie kept twice on the leaderboard that way is kept once.
- **Your other characters see this one as it is.** On an alt, the leaderboard showed your main as an old copy a guildie had passed on - days old, with a dash for assists - and nothing ever brought it up to date. Now each character keeps its own score on the board your other characters in the guild read, as it was when you last played it. Log in on the main once after the update to bring its row up to date.
- **A shorter right-click menu.** On an enemy: their Grudge file, the kill order, Track, and for an officer their guild on watch - the Grudgekeeper notes submenu is gone from there. On a player of your own side, Put on Shame and Give kudos are on the menu itself, each with its tags, and Their notes when anyone wrote one. A note in words, or a mark on an enemy, is written in the ledger: Mark someone on Most wanted's Marked tab, the action at the top of Shame or Kudos, or Add a note on their notes' card.
- **Reaper's splash is the scythe.** A scythe swings round and the seal lands on the strike, where the seal rose out of black smoke. Everyone is moved to the scythe once with this update. To have the smoke back, pick Smoke under Settings, Look, Reaper's splash.
- **Every splash and toast on one page.** The settings' Kill sprees, Warnings and Notes became three pages, Notifications, Sounds and Warnings. Notifications has your own and your guildies' side by side: kill sprees, new titles, kill orders fulfilled, revenge, sprees ended and tormentors put down, each Splash, Toast or Off. Warnings has who is near or joins your group, each On screen, Chat line or Off. Three presets set Notifications at once. All of it shows everything, Big moments shows all of your own and only the biggest from guildies, and Quiet shows your own as small toasts and nothing from guildies, for streaming. A guildie's sprees can be told from Reaper up, from Grudgebound up or only at 25, 50 and 100, and their titles from a title of your choice up. They can also be held to your own zone, and come without their sound. Everyone starts at Big moments for a guildie's: their sprees from Reaper up, their titles from Avenger up, and no toast when their spree ends. What you had switched off stays off. Your own new titles have a row of their own now, and start as your sprees were set. The nameplate marks and the map's pins moved to Around the game.
- **Grudges puts who you owe first.** It is one list now. The players you track come first, with an eye on their face, even at 0 - 0. Then come the players who lead over you, the one you owe most first, with "owed 3" under the score. Under a faint line come those you stand even with, and then those you lead, the closest to catching you first ("you lead 2"). The Tracked heading and the order you could click through (most fought, latest, A to Z) are gone, and so is the row for an enemy you only helped kill.
- **A row more where there is room.** Grudges, the You page and the kill orders page list ten rows to a page, down to the page arrows, where Grudges and You listed nine with a row's room left empty under them. Pages with column headings keep nine.
- **Revenge is told on screen.** Kill a player who has killed you since you last got them - the kill that earns the revenge point - and a splash says so: their face in a bronze ring, a red cut across it and blood behind it, REVENGE in blood red under it, and where the two of you stand, "Pølse Hans · now 3 - 5", their name in their class's colour and your number in gold. A guildie's revenge can come as a toast, the same picture smaller, with "Kari: revenge on Pølse Hans · now 1 - 2". It is off to begin with, because in a big guild somebody always has one. Both have a row on the Notifications page and a sound on Sounds. Big moments and All of it show yours as a splash, Quiet as a toast, and if you use Quiet it stays a toast. `/grudge spree revenge` shows both. A guildie's comes only from guildies on this version.
- **A warning when someone who leads over you is near.** A player who has killed you more often than you have killed them warns you when they come into sight, by the letter icon with the same sound as a kill order's target: "Pølse Hans is near - owed 2". Once you stand even it stops. It is on to begin with, under Settings, Warnings, "Someone who leads over me is near". One you track, or one with a kill order on them, warns as before.
- **A card's head stays clear of its seal.** A long line under the name, "Level 59 Windshaper Skyborne Warrior", ran under a kill order's seal. The name and that line now stop short of it, and the line goes on to a second one where it is long.
- **Everyone by name alone, and your guild's records stay yours after Forever's latest patch.** The patch stopped the game naming realms, and Grudgekeeper filed the guild under a new, empty name: the leaderboard and news started over and every open kill order arrived again as a letter. Now Grudgekeeper keeps every player, and your guild, by name alone - "Pølse Hans", the way the game itself names everyone now. At your next login what it kept moves over once. The records from that evening go back with the guild's own, the letters for orders you already had go, and orders that were new stay. A player it kept two or three times, once for each realm a guildie wrote them down with, becomes one, with their kills and deaths added up.
- **A dark band behind the words of every splash and toast.** The line under a splash and beside a toast stood white on the world itself, hard to read on snow or sunlit sand. Now a soft dark band lies behind it, as wide as its words, and fades in and out with them.
- **A spree ended shatters as a toast too.** As a toast, which is how your own spree's end comes unless you picked a splash, it showed only the broken seal. Now the burning seal pulses and bursts into its six shards there as well, smaller, and nothing is left behind, as on the splash.

## 0.1.0-beta1

Revenge - killing someone who killed you since you last got them - is worth 1 more, and so is avenging a guildie they killed in the last 15 minutes. Take the order, and you are also warned when they are near. Kill them and the order is yours: worth 3 points more, a screenshot is taken, and everyone who put gold on it is told what they owe you.

Not when someone already has an order on them, at most one a day per enemy, and only from a player who shares with the guild. On by default; the setting is in the Guild section.

It fades in and blinks when something lands, then rests with a faint glow until you read it. Your own kills make no sound, and letters landing together - the orders you missed, arriving as you log in - sound once. A kill order or a fulfilled one unrolls as a scroll; the rest - your points after a kill, who killed you, a bounty paid - is a short letter from the Grudgekeeper. When a target you took comes near, the scroll glows red, with a soft chime and one line under it.

An enemy gets a wanted poster: how often they killed the guild and whom, how often the guild got them, your own score with them, where they were last seen and the bounty on them, with a button to take the order or post one. A guildie gets their record: title and points, this week and all time, kill streak, kill orders fulfilled and the bounties they carried, nemesis.
