# Changelog

## 1.6.0

- **Kill sprees.** A spree is your kills worth points in a row, until an enemy player kills you. Grey and farmed kills don't count, and assists don't. Reaching 3, 5, 7, 10 and 15 in a row is announced - Bloodied, Merciless, Butcher, Reaper and Grudgebound - and Grudgebound again at every fifth after that. Your guildies' sprees are announced to you too.
- **A splash or a toast.** An announcement is a splash in the middle of the screen, with the tier's seal and its name on a banner, or a small toast by the letter icon. Each kind - your sprees, guildies' sprees, a spree that ended, a tormentor put down - is a splash, a toast or off, under Kill sprees in the settings. Two more settings say when they may come: always, not in combat, not in combat in a dungeon or raid, or never in a dungeon or raid. One that is held back waits, and comes afterwards. `/grudge spree` shows a sample of both.
- **A spree ended, and tormentors.** A spree of 3 or more that ends on a death to an enemy player is announced. So is the kill of a tormentor: an enemy who has killed three or more of the guild since any of you last killed them.
- **The Grudgekeeper's voice.** He calls out each tier. That is the tier's sound until you pick another: every tier, a spree that ended and a tormentor each have a sound to pick under Kill sprees. The voice lines and the spree sounds are a first version, and still being worked on.
- **Settings in a window of their own.** The settings are on the ledger's paper in a window of Grudgekeeper's own: a menu of chapters down the left, the chapter you picked beside it. A wax seal is a setting that is on, a dashed ring one that is off. They open in a fight, and no page scrolls. The ledger's Settings button, a right-click on the minimap button and `/grudge options` open them, and **Back to the ledger** takes you back. The game's own options page keeps one button that opens them too.
- **Arrange on screen.** The button at the foot of the settings, or `/grudge arrange`, shows the letter icon, the spree splash and the toasts with a frame around each: drag one where you want it, right-click it to put it back. The settings close while you do, and open again at Done. It replaces **Move the letter icon**.
- **"Duel to the death" is off the list of sounds.** It plays nothing in Forever. If it was your sound for a target near, you now have the Battleground horn.
- Kill sprees, their seals and banners and the Grudgekeeper's voice are a pull request from dakristia.

## 1.5.0

- **Tracked.** Your own list of enemies to keep an eye on. Use **Track someone** at the top of your Grudges, or **Track** in the right-click menu on an enemy. They sit at the top of your Grudges, the one seen latest first. When one of them is near, you get the same warning as for a kill order's target, and an eye over their nameplate. **Stop tracking** is on their poster and in the same menu. Nobody else sees the list. You can track 25 at most.

## 1.4.0

- **Assists.** When someone in your group lands the killing blow on an enemy player you hurt in that fight, it counts for you as an assist: the kill's points less one, at least one, and none for a grey or farmed kill. The game's damage meter tells who hit them once the fight is over, so an assist shows a moment after combat ends. Your pet's damage counts as yours. A mob's or a stranger's killing blow is no assist, because the meter only knows you and your group. Assists show in a new A column on the leaderboard, in your grudges, on the cards, in the guild's news and in the minimap button's tooltip. A guildie on an older Grudgekeeper shows "-" there. An assist that takes you to a new title brings its letter, as a kill does.
- **Old race portraits.** The faces are now drawn from the old character-creation screen in place of today's race icons, whichever skin you use. "Old race portraits" under Portraits in the settings switches back to today's icons. The Skyborne, whom the old screen never had, show their class crest.
- **Faces for enemies you never met.** A guildie's kill, death or kill order now carries the enemy's race and sex when they saw them, so the enemy's face shows in your ledger too.
- **Your score follows you to another PC.** Your score is kept on the computer you play on. Logging in on a second one started you from 0 there, and the guild's leaderboard showed 0. Going back to the first put the old score back, without what you did on the second. Now each PC keeps its own part of your score and the guild adds them up. When you log in, a guildie hands you back what your other PC made. This needs a guildie online with this version, and every PC you play on updated. A guildie on an older Grudgekeeper still shows whichever PC spoke last.
- **Avenging a guildie pays only in a later fight.** Killing or helping to kill an enemy who killed a guildie in the last 15 minutes is still worth a point more, but not in the fight the guildie died in. Leave combat and catch them again, and it counts. Before, one of the group dying first made every kill and assist on their killer a point more for the whole group.
- **What's new comes as a letter.** After an update the Grudgekeeper writes one short letter about it, to the first character you log in with. A new install gets none, and none comes with letters switched off.
- **How points are counted, on the leaderboard.** Hover the Points heading for the rules: what a kill is worth by level, the bonuses, the daily limit and what an assist gives.
- **The settings page and `/grudge help` show the version.** They showed a count of the changes since you logged in.
- Assists and the old portraits started as a pull request from dakristia.

## 1.3.0

- **Letters in the Grudgekeeper's own voice.** The small letters now read as the old keeper of the guild's grudges talking to you. A new title greets you by it - "Rise, Bloodhound" - tells you who fell and where, and ends with a word of his own for each title. A death, a bounty paid and a kill order that ran out are told the same way, and a kill order's scroll says they are wanted for crimes against the guild.
- **Unread letters fade.** A letter you haven't read fades from the letter icon after two minutes, so letters don't stand there blinking. It isn't lost: Letters in the ledger still opens it. "Letters wait until read" in the settings keeps them by the icon until you read them.
- **Most wanted shows a bounty in coins.** The bounty column read "85g" in red; it now shows the game's gold, silver and copper coins, as the kill orders page does. An order with no gold on it still says "order" in red.
- **Folded, the tabs hang down the ledger's right side, as icons.** One page wide, the tabs under the ledger were cut short to "Lead..." and "Most...". Folded, they now hang down its right side with an icon each, and the mouse over one shows its name. Open, they stay under it with their names.
- **A long kill order fits its scroll.** An order with a long note, several backers and where it was last seen ran on under the scroll's button, its seal and its bottom roll. The lines that matter least now give way first - who collects, the backers' names, where they were last seen - and the poster's words and the reward stay.
- **The page count shows beside Read all.** With two or more letters waiting, the Read all button covered the "1-9" of a long list's page count. The count and its arrows now sit just under the list.
- **A card's last line no longer runs into its buttons.** A line that wrapped onto a second line at the foot of a wanted poster ran into its buttons. A section whose line doesn't fit now gives way whole, heading and all.

## 1.2.0

- **Letters can be switched off.** "Letters" at the top of the settings' Letters section: off, nothing arrives as a letter, and the letter icon only shows for a warning. The ledger, the warnings and the mailbox card still tell you what matters.
- **Read all.** With two or more letters waiting, the ledger has a Read all button beside Letters, and Shift-click on the letter icon does the same: every letter read at once, and so gone.
- **Got together, told together.** When you get a kill order's target with others from your group close by, its letter says who was with you, and each of them gets the letter too, with a Tell the guild button of their own. The guild's news names everyone who was there.
- **A bounty got together is shared.** Everyone who got the target, the killing blow and the guildies with them, gets an equal part of the bounty, and everyone who put gold in pays each of them their part directly. The mailbox card, the letters and the order in the ledger show each part. A part paid by mail, by trade or marked by hand counts for that hunter, and each hunter gets a letter when theirs is paid.
- **Tell the guild from the ledger.** A letter you have read is gone, and with it the only Tell the guild button. The fulfilled order on the kill orders page now has one too, for whoever got them or was there. Told from either, neither offers it again.
- **A target near: a bubble you click to target them.** When the target of a kill order, or a player on your Marked list, is near, a bubble over the letter names them, and a click on it targets them. The game lets no addon set that click up during a fight, so a warning that comes in the middle of one tells you to target them by hand.
- **A horn instead of the chime, and a sound of your choice.** A target near, a Marked player near and the watched guild near now sound the game's battleground horn. A Sound dropdown under "When a kill order's target is near" in the settings picks another, or none. It sounds once in ten minutes for the same player or guild: one who steps out of sight and back is warned again, bubble and all, without the sound.
- **More sounds to pick from.** Both Sound dropdowns, for a target near and for a Shame player joining your group, now offer 18 of the game's own alert sounds where there were 5, among them Duel to the death, Invasion alert, Raid warning and Boss emote. Sounds other addons share through LibSharedMedia are still listed after them.
- **The mailbox reminds you of a bounty you owe.** When you open a mailbox owing a hunter their bounty, a card beside it lists them: who, how much and for whom. Fill in writes the letter for you, as money and never as C.O.D.; Paid marks one you settled another way.
- **A bounty is paid however you pay it.** Money you mail or trade to a hunter you owe is marked paid by itself, the oldest bounty first, and less than a whole bounty counts toward it. Before, only a letter Grudgekeeper had filled in counted.
- **Away when your order was fulfilled? You hear of it now.** If you were logged out when a guildie got the target of your kill order, or of one you put money into, the letter and the reminder to pay never came, and the order stayed open for you. The hunter's Grudgekeeper now says it again the next time you are both online.
- **Your own order is not a fulfilment.** Getting the target of your own kill order closes it, but it no longer counts as an order fulfilled on your profile, and its letter no longer promises you your own bounty by mail.
- **Lists no longer run on for ever.** Most wanted, the leaderboard, the open kill orders, Marked, your grudges, Lately, Shame, Kudos and the news show their first 25. Before, a list ran to everyone the guild had ever heard of. Your own kill order stays on the page however small its bounty, and the leaderboard shows your own place under the top 25. On Most wanted, Only mine picks from everyone, not from the 25: someone who killed you shows even when the guild has 25 worse. Sorted A to Z, a list is still its 25 most important, put in that order.
- **Closed kill orders fold away.** They sit under a Closed orders heading on the kill orders page, folded: a click on it shows the latest 10, another hides them. A closed order that still needs you stays in sight: one you owe a bounty on, or one you got and haven't told the guild about yet. Sorting the page by name or bounty sorts the open orders among themselves. Before, a fulfilled or withdrawn order was mixed in with them. The closed orders stay under their own heading, and so do Marked and the watched guild.

## 1.1.0

- **Names in Cyrillic show.** A character named in Russian letters showed up as nothing, or as empty boxes, in the ledger, on the cards and in the letters, because Grudgekeeper's two typefaces hold only the Latin alphabet. Its text now falls back to the game's own Cyrillic faces, as the game's windows do, and to its Korean and Chinese ones too.
- **Typing such a name works.** In the box that asks who, a name typed in Cyrillic or with ø, æ or å gets its capital letter as the game writes it ("петя" becomes "Петя"), and searching finds a name whatever case you type it in. Before, the box turned a Cyrillic name down.
- **The ledger's tabs hang from the book.** Their tops now sit behind the frame's bottom edge, and they have the same heavy double border as the frame, so they no longer look like a row of buttons set under the window.
- **Officers can type in the guild to watch.** Before, a guild went on watch only from the right-click menu on one of its players, so a guild nobody had met lately could not be watched at all. Officers and the guild master can now type it in with `/grudge guild`, or with the "watch a guild" link on the Watched guild heading of the kill orders page once there is an order up: the guild's name, in any case. The first time an officer meets one of its players, the game's own spelling replaces what was typed.
- **No screenshot unless you ask for one.** A new install no longer takes a screenshot when you fulfil a kill order. Tick "When I fulfil a kill order" under Screenshots in the settings to have one. If you already had it on, it stays on.
- **Close instead of Later on the letters.** A letter about your new title, a death, a payment or an expired order had a Later button, which kept the letter unread, so the letter icon came straight back. It is a Close button now: the letter has been read, and it goes.
- **A sound of your choice when someone on your Shame list joins your group.** The settings' Notes section has a Sound dropdown under that warning: the chime it has always made to begin with, the raid warning, the ready check and a few more of the game's own, or None. The guild YippYapp also has a Norwegian clip of its own, "Måkke ta med han", and starts with that. Any sound another addon has registered with LibSharedMedia is listed too. Picking one plays it, so you hear what you chose, and several joining at once make one sound.

## 0.1.0-beta4

- **Forget a grudge.** A player you have a grudge with has a Forget grudge button on their card; click it twice to confirm. It takes your record of them away: the grudge, its lines in your log, your kills and deaths in the guild's Most wanted and your news about them. The guild is asked to drop your part too, and only your part: what a guildie wrote down about them stays. Your score stays as it was. Before, a wrong grudge was there for good, like a death a hunter dealt that was written down as a shaman's before the death recap was fixed.
- **The leaderboard leaves out guildies under level 10.** Alts parked in a starting zone filled it with rows of nothing. Their score is kept, and they are back on the board at level 10. Your place ("#3 of 12") counts the same board.
- **A kill order's scroll has a bar of buttons under it.** Open kill orders closes the scroll and opens the ledger on that very order; Close rolls it up. They replace the small X on the top roll, which was hard to hit, and the "1 of 3" with its arrows: a scroll shows one order, and the next letter opens with the next click on the letter icon. The Hitlist button on the scroll is gone, as Open kill orders does its job.
- **Letters no longer pile up.** A letter you have read goes when you close the letters. Before, read letters stayed for three days, so the pile soon said "10 of 10", every one of them saying what the ledger says anyway. While the letters are open, the ones you read stay so you can page back. Press Later to keep the letter you are on: it stays, unread. With no letters waiting, the Letters button in the ledger is greyed out.
- **Watch an enemy guild.** An officer or the guild master can right-click a player from the other side and pick Watch their guild. From then on, when anyone in your guild comes across someone from that guild, the letter icon glows red with a soft chime and the line under it names the guild and who of theirs is near. It warns once a minute at most, and a group that shows up together gets one warning. The guild watches one enemy guild at a time: watching another replaces it. It shows on the Kill orders page under Watched guild, with who put it there, and stays watched when that officer logs out.
- **Names without realms.** Names are shown as first name and surname everywhere, never with a realm after them. A guildie's nemesis showed as "Kiire Elää-CAMWOWEU5Realm2PvP" when that guildie played on another of the server's realms.
- **One picture per player.** Everyone wears their race's face with their class on its shoulder, or their class crest when Grudgekeeper doesn't know their race. A player's own face, taken when you targeted or moused over them, is gone. It lasted only until you logged out, so the same player looked one way in one place and another way somewhere else, and changed at every reload.
- **A letter only when a kill earns you a new title.** Every kill used to send you a letter, which after a busy evening was a pile of them. Now only the kill that makes you a Cutthroat, a Bloodhound and so on does, and the letter is short: your new title, the kill that did it, and your points and place in the guild. Every kill still counts for points, and the ledger's Grudges and News list them all.
- **See who put gold in the pot.** A kill order's card has a new section, The pot: who put gold in and how much, the most first. On a fulfilled order it also says who has paid. Before, the bounty only said "from 2 guildies".
- **A backer's gold no longer drops out of the bounty when they log out.** When you log in, the guild now also passes on what others put in each open order's pot. Before, if you logged in while a backer was offline, you saw only the poster's part of the bounty until that backer came online again.
- **Long lines fit.** A line too long for its row in the ledger steps down a size, then uses first names only, and if it still doesn't fit, hover over it to read it whole. "Hellig Bro added to the pot on..." lost the rest of its line in the news. The lines under a guildie's Lately on their card step down a size too.
- **The rows start further left.** Only the leaderboard keeps room for a rank in front of the face. On every other page the rows sat far to the right, and names and the lines under them were cut sooner than they had to be.
- **The score on the Grudges page is easier to read.** The number that isn't leading and the dash between them were a pale brown that nearly vanished into the page. They're in the page's darker ink now, in the game's plain type.
- **Your own kill orders say "by you"** in the list, as their card does, not your full name.

## 0.1.0-beta3

- **Nothing is lost when a guildie logs out.** When you log in, the guild answers with what you missed, and that now includes what belongs to guildies who are offline: their open kill orders and their rows on the leaderboard. Before, an order only reached you if its poster was online at the same time as you, so an order posted while you were away could pass you by. A guildie who logs in a few seconds after another gets an answer too (they got none before, for kill orders and notes alike), and a long answer is no longer cut off halfway.

- **The cards in the ledger, redone.** Every number has a tile of its own, with what it counts and a line that says something about it.
  - **A guildie's record:** points, kills and deaths, each with this week's under it, and their place on the board out of how many. There's a bar to their next title, then their highlights, those they have: kill streak and their best, their nemesis, the orders they fulfilled and the bounties on them. Under those, the last few things the news says they did. The top three of the week wear their wax seal on the card too.
  - **An enemy's poster:** the bounty and how many put gold in it, then their score against the guild like a scoreboard: their kills on us and their deaths to us, each with when it last happened. Then how long ago they were last seen, and where. Under the tiles come the order (who posted it and when, why, when it runs out) and their history: your own score with them, whom they killed and who killed them.
  - **Notes** on a player get a section of their own.
- **Faces.** Target or mouse over a guildie, or an enemy the guild has a record of, and their poster, their card and their rows in the ledger show their own face, with their class on its shoulder. The game draws a face only while the player is there, so it lasts until you log out or reload.
- **Their race's face, kept.** For an enemy the guild has a record of (a grudge, a kill order, a kill or a death, a note), Grudgekeeper keeps the race and sex it saw. After a reload they wear their race's face instead of their class crest. A trip to the barber is picked up the next time you see them.
- **Nobody takes a kill order any more.** An order is the whole message: the guild wants this one dead, kill them on sight. Take the order is gone from the poster and the letter, and so is the Hunting line. Any guildie's kill fulfils an order and earns its bounty, as before.
  - **Everyone is warned about every order.** When the target of any open kill order is near you, the letter icon glows red with a soft chime. When a guildie sees them, a quiet line says who saw them and where. Before, that was only for orders you took or posted. The setting is now called "When a kill order's target is near", and "For every open kill order" is gone.
  - **Where they were seen** is on the kill orders page too, under the name: "seen in Wetlands, 5 min ago".
- **The Grudges page reads like a scoreboard.** Each grudge shows its score as "2 - 1", with the leader's number in colour and "you lead", "they lead" or "even" under it. Before, it was two bare columns of numbers under YOU and THEY. Under the name is what happened last, and where: "killed you 11 min ago · Ashenvale". The heading row over the page is gone, and the list starts right under the page's title. To change the order, click the word beside Your grudges: most fought, latest or A to Z.
- **Marked lives on the kill orders page.** The enemies marked to kill on sight are listed under their own heading at the foot of the Kill orders page, instead of on a tab of their own. Click "mark someone" beside the heading to mark another; /grudge notes opens that page.
- **Shame and Kudos sit at the right** of the window, apart from the enemy's pages.
- **Most wanted has a page of its own.** It lists everyone who has killed the guild, how often, and the bounty on them now. Sort by any of those, or tick Only mine for the ones who killed you. The leaderboard is the guild's scores alone, so it no longer runs onto a second page.
- **This week's leaderboard is never half empty.** When only a few have scored this week, the rest of the guild fills its first page at 0, the best of all time first. Your place this week still counts only those who scored.
- **A page turns a whole page.** After 1-9 of 10, the next page showed 2-10, with eight rows again; now it shows the tenth. The mouse wheel turns pages too, and a heading never sits alone at the foot of a page.
- **The kill orders page is never blank.** With no open order it lists who has killed the guild most, and the first of them is on the right with Post a kill order. With nobody yet, you see your own record.
- **Every player under their own name, surname and all.** On the live servers, a player on another of the server's realms was kept as "Unc-Shark", with the surname taken for a realm. Every Unc then looked like the same player: a hunter's Arcane Shot was put on a shaman's name, and a kill order on one Unc could mark another. Names are now kept and shown as the game writes them, "Unc Shark", and what was kept the old way is moved over the first time you log in.
- **A letter only for a death that matters.** Dying once no longer sends you a letter. The death that puts a kill order on your killer still does: the third of the guild they kill in two hours, or the third time they kill you in half an hour. Every death still counts, and the ledger has it under Grudges and News.
- **A death goes to the one who killed you.** It goes to the player the death recap names. When the recap leaves the surname off and more than one player you saw could be it, it is counted against nobody.
- **Kill orders from guildies on the server's other realms** name their target without the poster's realm, the same as yours.
- **Rows that say who in full.** On the Grudges page, a death's spell comes at the end of the line under the name. In the column beside it, a long spell like Judgement of Righteousness cut the name to "Slain by I...". In the News, your own lines say You, so whom you killed fits.
- **Last seen counts the news too.** A guildie's kill or death with an enemy says where they were, so the poster in the ledger can tell you where and when they were last seen, even without a kill order or a sighting.

## 0.1.0-beta2

- **The window is a ledger now.** A sheet of parchment in the game's own frame, with the game's tabs under it, and the thin inner frame of the game's own profession window round it, a line between its two sides: the table on the left - kill orders, the leaderboard, your grudges, the news - and on the right the row you clicked: an enemy's wanted poster with Take the order and Add to the pot, a guildie's record. The numbers that matter sit side by side at the top of the card - the bounty, its backers and who hunts them; how often they killed us and we them; a guildie's points, kills and deaths - and under them what happened, and why the order was posted when its poster wrote why, in two columns, with the buttons right below. The rows are easier to read: names in the game's plain type, every other row shaded, the chosen one marked in red, and an enemy's level, race and class after their name. The red arrow button beside the X folds it to one page, as the spells panel's does; a click on a row then turns the page, and Back turns it back. A long list turns a page at a time, or scrolls with the wheel.
- **A settings page you can read.** Every setting has a short name, and what it does is in its (i); the pages of help text and the lists of commands under them are gone.
- **Two skins.** Parchment, the Grudgekeeper's own, or Classic: the game's dark panels in gold and white, like the game's own windows. Pick one under Skin in the settings and the ledger, the note and the window that asks who switch at once, no reload. The letters and the kill order's scroll are the Grudgekeeper's paper in both.
- **Faces fill their rings.** A face sat a little short of its bronze ring, with a sliver of page showing between them; it reaches under the ring now, and the ring is a duller bronze, less shiny beside the page.
- **A place on the board says what it counts.** A record said "#1 of 4 in the guild" over a leaderboard that, this week, listed only one: the four were everyone all time. It now reads "#1 of 4 all time, #1 of 1 this week".
- **A letter's stamp no longer hides it.** The FULFILLED on an order complete is stamped under the writing, half faded, instead of over it.
- **Faces in the ledger.** A guildie shows their race's portrait with their class on its shoulder - it comes with their score, so they need this version too; an enemy shows their class, and on their poster their face once you have seen them this session. The top three of the week wear wax seals of gold, silver and copper.
- **A close button on everything.** An iron cross of Grudgekeeper's own on the kill order's scrolls - the one that arrives and the one you write; the ledger, the letters, the profile cards and the notes have the game's own X.
- **Your own kills wait a minute.** The letter about your kill - or about an order your kill fulfilled - goes into the pile at once, but the letter icon shows it only a minute after the kill and once you are out of combat, so nothing blinks at you the moment a fight ends. Another kill in that minute joins the same letter.
- **A Grudgekeeper tab on the Inspect window.** Inspect a guildie and a tab below the game's own opens their page of the ledger - title, points, their week, kill streak, nemesis - right in the Inspect window. A guildie with no score yet says so. An enemy, if the game lets you inspect one, gets their wanted poster, and the tab glows while there is a kill order on them. Anyone else gets no tab, and the tab can be switched off.
- **Kill orders from the right-click menu.** Right-click an enemy player and pick Post a kill order, right below Grudgekeeper profile - no need to open the ledger. When a guildie already has an order on them the line says Add to the pot, and on your own order Change your kill order.
- **Your own orders warn you too.** The red glow, the chime and the line under the letter used to come only for orders you took - and your own can't be taken. Now an order you posted warns you as well, and so does one your death posted, so a player camping you can't walk back up unannounced. The setting is now "When my targets are near", under Warnings.
- **Notes on players** (from Guild Grudge, by dakristia). The ledger has three more tabs, one per list. Marked (for an enemy you want to mark without a bounty or a history) sits after Grudges, before News; Shame and Kudos sit a step apart, after the enemy's pages. Right-click any player - friend or enemy - and pick Grudgekeeper notes to write a note, or put them on Shame or Kudos with a tag (Ninja looter, Scammer, Helpful, Great healer, or one of your own; every tag has a small icon wherever it is shown). Notes are the guild's: you see what guildies wrote, and you can vote a note up or down. You can only change or delete your own, and moving a player only moves your own mark.
- **Add someone straight from the ledger, target or not.** Every page you can add to has its own button at the top - Post a kill order, Mark someone, Put someone on Shame, Give someone kudos. It asks who: your target; for a kill order or a mark, the enemies you saw in the last 15 minutes and your grudges; for Shame and Kudos, your group and whoever left it in the last two hours - the ninja looter is usually gone by the time you want to write them down. Type to search everyone the guild keeps notes on, or a name nobody has met. /grudge post with no enemy targeted asks the same way.
- **Sorting by the column headings.** Click a column heading - the name, Bounty, You, They or Votes - to sort a page by it, and again for the page's own order; the heading in use is underlined. Only mine, a box under the page's title, keeps just your own rows. Letters and Settings are buttons at the foot of the list - Letters with how many are unread.
- **Writing a kill order or a note.** A kill order is written on a scroll: the player's face and name at the head, lines to write on, and the red wax seal to send it. A note, and the window that asks who, are parchment in the game's own frame, with its own boxes and buttons: the list and a tag are a click each, every tag with its small icon, and a tag's icon no longer runs into the next one. When a note can't be written, the window says why. Opened from the ledger, they lie on it: drag one and the ledger comes along, close the ledger and they close with it.
- **A quiet word on who you meet.** A player you have notes on gets a line on their tooltip and a small dim mark beside their nameplate, a cross, an exclamation or a tick for the list. When someone on your Shame list joins your group you get a warning with their tag. A Marked or Shame player near you, and a Kudos player joining your group, can tell you too, but only if you turn that on. Every setting is in the new Notes section.
- **Notes on the cards.** The wanted poster and a guildie's record show how many notes there are on that player and the newest one.
- **Notes from the chat box.** /grudge note writes a note on your target, or on a name; /grudge shame, kudos and move do the rest, and /grudge notes opens Marked, and /grudge shame and kudos with no name open their pages. /grudge watch still works for shame. If you used Guild Grudge, /grudge import takes over its notes and lists, and /gg works as it did when Guild Grudge isn't running.
- **Guildies on the server's other realms count.** On the live servers one guild spans several of the server's realms, and when a guildie on another of them took a kill order, saw its target, put gold in its pot, paid their share or fulfilled it, nobody on another realm heard - not even the one who posted it, so a fulfilled order could stay open and its bounty never fall due. A vote on a note was lost the same way, and a note or a mark on a guildie came apart into a second player. They all land on the right order, note and player now.
- **Turning between kill orders works.** The arrows and "1 of 3" on a kill order scroll sat behind its bottom roll, where they could not be clicked; they are on the roll now.
- **Long letters stay clear of the seal.** A short letter from the Grudgekeeper still ends with his last word; a long one - a kill with revenge, a streak and a new title, or a death that posted a kill order - leaves it out, so the text never runs into the seal and the signature.

## 0.1.0-beta1

The first version, a beta: nothing in it has met a real fight yet.

- **Your world PvP, counted.** Every enemy player you (or your pet) kill in the open world, with their level, class and race, and everyone who kills you - how often, and with what. The Grudges page lists everyone you have a history with, and your nemesis: the one who has killed you most.
- **Points.** A kill is worth what their level is to you: green 1, yellow 2, orange 3, red 4, grey nothing. Revenge - killing someone who killed you since you last got them - is worth 1 more, and so is avenging a guildie they killed in the last 15 minutes. The same player is worth points twice a day; after that, nothing, bonuses included.
- **Titles**, from Unblooded through Cutthroat, Bloodhound, Headhunter, Slayer, Executioner and Grudgebearer to Dread Reaper at 800 points. Your kill streak - kills without dying - and your best one are kept too.
- **The guild's leaderboard**, this week or all time; the week starts over at the weekly reset. **Most wanted**: the enemy players who have killed your guild most, and whom.
- **Kill orders.** Put a price on an enemy player's head - a bounty in gold, or for honour. Your guild gets the order as a letter, and the target carries a red skull seal over their nameplate. Take the order, and you are also warned when they are near. Kill them and the order is yours: worth 3 points more, a screenshot is taken, and everyone who put gold on it is told what they owe you.
- **A pot per target.** Anyone can chip in: Add to the pot on an order, or post on someone who already has an order on them, and your share goes into that order instead of making a second one. The order shows who put in what. Each backer owes their own share to whoever fulfils it.
- **Orders that post themselves.** When one enemy kills three of the guild in two hours, or the same guildie three times in half an hour, the death that makes it three posts a kill order on them: no gold, for the guild's honour, for three days. The order says why ("Killed Kari, Edvard and Lars in two hours"), and anyone can take it or add to its pot. Not when someone already has an order on them, at most one a day per enemy, and only from a player who shares with the guild. On by default; the setting is in the Guild section.
- **Paying a bounty.** No gold is taken when you post. At a mailbox, Pay bounty fills in the letter for each share you owe; you press Send, and that share is marked paid. The hunter gets a letter for every payment.
- **Letters, never popups.** News arrives as a small sealed letter, the way the game shows new mail - but wherever you put it, not on the minimap: drag it anywhere, or place it with /grudge notice. It fades in and blinks when something lands, then rests with a faint glow until you read it. Your own kills make no sound, and letters landing together - the orders you missed, arriving as you log in - sound once. A kill order or a fulfilled one unrolls as a scroll; the rest - your points after a kill, who killed you, a bounty paid - is a short letter from the Grudgekeeper. When a target you took comes near, the scroll glows red, with a soft chime and one line under it.
- **Profiles.** Right-click a player - the target frame, party and raid frames, a name in chat, the guild roster - and pick Grudgekeeper profile, or click a name in the window. An enemy gets a wanted poster: how often they killed the guild and whom, how often the guild got them, your own score with them, where they were last seen and the bounty on them, with a button to take the order or post one. A guildie gets their record: title and points, this week and all time, kill streak, kill orders fulfilled and the bounties they carried, nemesis. Anyone else's menu is left alone, and the line can be switched off.
- **Last seen, on the map.** When a guildie sees the target of an open order, the same red seal goes on the world map where they stood, for 15 minutes; click it for the wanted poster. Your own spot is sent with what you see only while you share with the guild.
- **News**, a page of what the guild has been up to: kills, deaths, orders posted, backed and fulfilled. On an ORDER COMPLETE letter, Tell the guild puts one line in guild chat, for guildies without Grudgekeeper - only when you click it.
- **Quiet extras**: one line on the tooltip of an enemy you have a history with, and a small badge beside the guild window that opens the leaderboard. Both can be switched off.
- **Horde and Alliance alike**, and any guild: each guild has its own board and orders.
- **/grudge test** puts four sample letters in your pile, to see what they look like.
- **/grudge debug** opens a small test panel for this beta: buttons that make each part happen - a kill, a death, a guildie's order, a sighting on the map, a bounty owed - so all of it can be tried before there are real fights. Nothing reaches your guild while it is open, and closing it puts your record and the guild's back as they were. It goes again once Grudgekeeper has been tried for real.
